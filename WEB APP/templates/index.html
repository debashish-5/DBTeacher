Design & Performance Recommendations

Images

  • Use responsive srcset + sizes for hero & carousel images.
  • Accept modern codecs (WebP / AVIF) and provide JPEG fallback.
  • Defer non-LCP images & lazy-load with loading="lazy".

Animations & UX

  • Animate transforms & opacity only — keep will-change minimal.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion for accessibility.
  • Limit simultaneous heavy animations on lower-end devices.
